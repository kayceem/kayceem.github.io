## About Me

I am passionate about Natural Language Processing and Artificial Intelligence, with a focus on advancing the state-of-the-art in language understanding and generation. My research interests lie at the intersection of deep learning and computational linguistics, exploring how machines can better comprehend and produce human language.

I am particularly interested in large language models, multilingual NLP, and developing AI systems that can effectively process and reason about textual information. Through my work, I aim to contribute to the development of more robust, interpretable, and ethically-aligned AI technologies that can benefit society.

## Research Interests

My research focuses on several key areas within Natural Language Processing and AI:

- **Large Language Models**: Investigating the capabilities, limitations, and potential applications of large-scale language models, including optimization techniques and efficient training methods
- **Multilingual NLP**: Developing models and techniques that can effectively work across multiple languages, particularly low-resource languages
- **Language Understanding**: Advancing machine comprehension of natural language through improved semantic understanding and contextual reasoning
- **Text Generation**: Exploring methods for generating coherent, contextually appropriate, and factually accurate text
- **AI Ethics & Fairness**: Ensuring that AI systems are developed responsibly with considerations for bias, fairness, and societal impact
