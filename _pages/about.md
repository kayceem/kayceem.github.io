## About Me

I am passionate about Natural Language Processing and Artificial Intelligence, with a focus on advancing the state-of-the-art in language understanding and generation. My research interests lie at the intersection of deep learning and computational linguistics, exploring how machines can better comprehend and produce human language.

I am particularly interested in large language models, multilingual NLP, and developing AI systems that can effectively process and reason about textual information. Through my work, I aim to contribute to the development of more accessible, interpretable, and trustworthy AI technologies that can benefit society.

## Research Interests

My research interests are several key areas within Natural Language Processing and AI:

- **Deep Learning**
- **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**
- **Large Language Models (LLMs)**
- **Generative AI**
- **Trustworthy AI**
- **Computer Vision**
- **Multimodal Learning**
